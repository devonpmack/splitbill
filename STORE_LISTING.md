## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Evenpot makes it easy to share costs with the people you live, travel and hang out with. Add what you spent, choose how to split it, and Evenpot keeps track of who owes whom.

Evenpot is free, and it stays free. There are no daily limits on how many expenses you can add, no ads, and no paid Pro tier hiding the useful parts.

SPLIT IT YOUR WAY
- Split equally, by exact amounts or by percentages
- Add an expense to a group or to a single friend
- Edit an expense if you got it wrong

KNOW WHO OWES WHAT
- See your balance with every friend and every group at a glance
- Simplify debts so a group settles up in the fewest possible payments
- Record a payment when someone settles up, and balances update right away

GROUPS AND FRIENDS
- Make a group for a trip, a household or a night out and invite people by email
- Keep a list of friends for the one-off expenses that do not belong to a group
- An activity feed shows what changed and when

MADE TO BE USED EVERY DAY
- Updates show up live for everyone in a group
- View balances in the currency you prefer
- Light and dark themes
- Choose which alerts you want to receive

Sign up with your email address and start splitting in a minute. Evenpot is made by Devin Software.
```

- **Features.** Everything listed exists in the app at this commit. If the recurring and future-dated transactions work lands later, add a line for it in the update that ships it.
